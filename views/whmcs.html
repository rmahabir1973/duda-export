WHMCS: how customers pay

A read-only copy of WHMCS, for planning the move to Stripe. The portal can only read from WHMCS, never change it. Admins only. It refreshes by itself twice a day, or press Sync now.

    By payment kind

    "Mainly" counts each customer once, under the kind most of their active services use. "Any" counts a customer under every kind they use, so it adds up to more than the total. Invoice figures are the last 12 months.
